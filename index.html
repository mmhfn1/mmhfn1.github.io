Who I am

I'm finishing an MSc in Data Science at Heriot-Watt University, Edinburgh — with a background in software engineering and a compulsion to build things driven entirely by curiosity: chess engines, tournament simulators, gravitational wave analysis pipelines.

What sets me apart is that I gravitate toward the work most people skip: the cleaning, the curation, the unglamorous craft of making a dataset trustworthy before a single model ever sees it. I also create and collate datasets entirely in my own time — not as preparation for something else, but because I find the craft of it genuinely absorbing.

The course Biologically Inspired Computation changed how I see data entirely — watching evolution, swarms, and neural architectures operate as nature's own optimisation strategies. That lens follows me into everything I build.
